# QuickCart Internal Knowledge Base

## Refund policy
Refunds for returned items are issued to the original payment method within
5-7 business days after the return is received at our warehouse. Items can be
returned within 30 days of delivery if unused and in original packaging.

## Duplicate charges and billing errors
If a customer is charged twice for the same order, the duplicate charge is a
billing error. Support can raise a priority refund for the duplicate amount
immediately. The duplicate amount is returned to the card within 3-5 business
days. Many banks show the duplicate as a "pending" hold that drops off
automatically in 2-3 days. Always share the refund reference number with the
customer. A customer does not need to raise a chargeback; if they already did,
the bank chargeback process may take longer (up to 30 days).

## Shipping and delivery times
Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days in metro cities and 5-8 business
days elsewhere. If tracking shows "In transit" with no update for more than 5
days, the parcel is treated as delayed and support opens an investigation with
the courier (result within 48 hours). If the parcel cannot be located, the
customer can choose a free replacement shipped by express delivery or a full
refund. For delayed gift orders support may also add a Rs 250 goodwill credit.

## Account login and OTP problems
OTP codes are valid for 5 minutes. "Invalid OTP" errors are often caused by
requesting several codes in a row - only the most recent code works. After 3
failed attempts the account is locked for 30 minutes for security and unlocks
automatically. Support can unlock business accounts manually after verifying
the registered phone number. Customers should update the app to the latest
version.

## QuickCart Plus membership and cancellation
QuickCart Plus costs Rs 999 per year and includes free express delivery and
early sale access. Members can cancel anytime from Account > Membership >
Cancel. After cancelling, benefits continue until the end of the paid period.
Partial refunds are given only if no Plus benefits were used in the last 30
days; otherwise the membership is not refunded but will not renew.

## Business accounts and bulk pricing
Business accounts get tiered discounts: 5% off for monthly spend above
Rs 25,000, 8% above Rs 50,000 and 12% above Rs 1,00,000. Verified businesses
with a GST number can pay by invoice with 30-day payment terms. The sales team
(sales@quickcart.com) sets up business accounts within 2 business days and
assigns a dedicated account manager.

## Feature requests and feedback
All feature requests are logged and shared with the product team every week.
We cannot promise release dates for new features. Wishlist sharing is on the
product roadmap but has no confirmed release date. Positive feedback is shared
with the delivery and warehouse teams.

## Escalation
Angry customers, chargeback threats and repeated complaints must be escalated
to a senior support specialist, who will personally follow up within 4 hours.
